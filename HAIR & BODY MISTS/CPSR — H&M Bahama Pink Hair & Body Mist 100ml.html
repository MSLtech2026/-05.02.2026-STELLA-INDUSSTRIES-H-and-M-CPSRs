Microbiological Risk Justification
This product contains 65.00% w/w Ethyl Alcohol 95%. Per ISO 29621, formulations with ethanol concentrations exceeding 20% are considered "low risk" for microbial growth. Additionally, the formula incorporates 0.30% Euxyl PE 9010 (Phenoxyethanol and Ethylhexylglycerin) as a validated broad-spectrum preservative system. The combination of high alcohol content and chemical preservation renders the product inhospitable to bacteria, yeasts, and moulds. Formal challenge testing (ISO 11930) is not required for this product type.

3. Reasoning

Basis for Safety Conclusion

The safety assessment of H&M Bahama pink Hair & Body Perfume Mist 100 ml has been conducted in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 (the EU Cosmetics Regulation), specifically Annex I. The assessment is based on all available data relating to the product formula SIL/MFHBM/DF/004-00, physicochemical properties, stability, microbiological quality, packaging, toxicological profiles, and exposure assessment.

Formulation: The product is an aqueous-alcoholic hair and body perfume mist consisting of Ethyl Alcohol (65.00%), Purified Water (27.70%), Fragrance (5.00%), and a validated solubiliser and preservative system. All ingredients are established cosmetic components with well-documented safety profiles. The formulation does not contain any ingredients prohibited under Annex II, nor does it exceed restricted substance limits in Annex III or V. Phenoxyethanol is used at ~0.27%, well within the 1.0% limit of Annex V.

Fragrance safety: The fragrance mixture 'Pink Tropical Mist' (P02X844) is IFRA 51st Amendment compliant at the 5.00% usage level for Category 2 (Body Mist) and Category 7B (Leave-on hair spray). Identified allergens Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone and Benzyl Salicylate are managed through IFRA compliance and mandatory labeling per Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009.

Toxicological profile: All ingredients were assessed for systemic exposure (SED) based on a conservative 2 g/day application rate. The resulting Margin of Safety (MoS) exceeds 100 in all cases (lowest MoS = 120 for Ethanol), demonstrating acceptable safety. No CMR or prohibited substances are present.

Stability and microbiological quality: The product demonstrated excellent stability over 6 months under accelerated conditions (40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5%; Batch 251225; MFG 25/06/2025 → 25/12/2025). All parameters—appearance, colour, odour, alcohol content (65.14–65.37%), specific gravity (0.870–0.875), vacuum leak, and packaging—remained within specification throughout. High ethanol content (65.22%) supplemented by Euxyl PE 9010 (Phenoxyethanol + Ethylhexylglycerin) ensures the product is hostile to microbial growth; challenge testing is not required per ISO 29621.

Flammability: The product is classified as a Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) due to the ethanol content and a flash point of ~21°C. Comprehensive hazard labelling and safe handling instructions are applied.
