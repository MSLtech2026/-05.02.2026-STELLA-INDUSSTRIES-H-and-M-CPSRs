Microbiological Risk Justification
This product contains 65.00% w/w Ethyl Alcohol 95%. Per ISO 29621, formulations with ethanol concentrations exceeding 20% are considered "low risk" for microbial growth. Additionally, the formula incorporates 0.30% Euxyl PE 9010 (Phenoxyethanol and Ethylhexylglycerin) as a validated broad-spectrum preservative system. The combination of high alcohol content and chemical preservation renders the product inhospitable to bacteria, yeasts, and moulds. Formal challenge testing (ISO 11930) is not required for this product type.

7. Exposure to the Cosmetic Product

The systemic exposure dose (SED) is calculated using an adapted SCCS framework for leave-on hair and body perfume mists, in accordance with the SCCS Notes of Guidance for the Testing of Cosmetic Ingredients and their Safety Evaluation, 11th revision (2021).

Exposure ParameterValue
Areas of ApplicationHair and body skin (neck, shoulders, wrists, décolleté, hair)
Product CategoryHair & Body Mist
Product TypeLeave-on
Duration and FrequencyDaily use
Retention Factor (RF)1.0 (leave-on product — full dermal/hair retention assumed)
Estimated Amount of Product Applied per Day2000 mg/day (2 g/day; conservative estimate for perfume mist)
Target GroupAdults
Body Weight (BW)60 kg (conservative SCCS adult default)
Calculated Relative Daily Exposure33.33 mg product / kg bw / day
Calculation Formula
SED (mg/kg bw/day) = (Amount applied [mg/day] × Ingredient concentration [fraction] × Retention Factor) / Body Weight [kg]

Relative Daily Exposure (total product):
= (2,000 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 33.33 mg product / kg bw / day

Note on Exposure: The applied amount of 2 g/day is a conservative estimate designed to reflect the broader application surface of a hair and body mist compared to a fine fragrance (0.24 g/day) while remaining distinct from the higher default for deodorants (2.8 g/day). This ensures a robust safety margin for all leave-on ingredients including fragrance allergens.

3. Reasoning

Basis for Safety Conclusion

The safety assessment of H&M Hair & Body Mist - Sun Kissed Caramel 100 ml has been conducted in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 (the EU Cosmetics Regulation), specifically Annex I. The assessment is based on all available data relating to the product formula SIL/MFHBM/SKC/001-00, physicochemical properties, stability, microbiological quality, packaging, toxicological profiles, and exposure assessment.

Formulation: The product is a six-ingredient aqueous-alcoholic hair and body perfume mist. All ingredients are established cosmetic components with well-documented safety profiles. The formulation does not contain any ingredients prohibited under Annex II, nor does it exceed restricted substance limits in Annex III or V. Phenoxyethanol is used at ~0.27%, well within the 1.0% limit.

Fragrance safety: The fragrance mixture 'Waffle Cone' (P00W384) is IFRA 51st Amendment compliant at the 5.00% usage level for Category 2 (Body Mist) and Category 7B (Leave-on hair spray). Three allergens (HEXAMETHYLINDANOPYRAN, VANILLIN, and TETRAMETHYL ACETYLOCTAHYDRONAPHTHALENES) exceed the 0.001% leave-on threshold and are declared on the label per Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1545.

Toxicological profile: All ingredients were assessed for systemic exposure (SED) based on a conservative 2 g/day application rate. The resulting Margin of Safety (MoS) exceeds 100 in all cases (lowest MoS = 120 for Ethanol), demonstrating acceptable safety. No CMR or prohibited substances are present.

Stability and microbiological quality: The product demonstrated excellent stability over 6 months under accelerated conditions (40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5%; Batch 251222; MFG 25/06/2025 → 25/12/2025). All parameters—appearance, colour, odour, alcohol content (65.17–65.41%), specific gravity (0.867–0.877), vacuum leak, and packaging—remained within specification throughout. High ethanol content (65.00%) supplemented by Euxyl PE 9010 ensures the product is hostile to microbial growth; challenge testing is not required per ISO 29621.

Flammability: The product is classified as a Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) due to the 65% ethanol content. Comprehensive hazard labelling and safe handling instructions are applied.
