The product is considered safe when used according to instructions; any misuse beyond these parameters is addressed by standard regulatory warnings for flammable fine fragrances.

7. Exposure to the Cosmetic Product

Exposure assessment follows the SCCS Notes of Guidance for the Testing of Cosmetic Ingredients and their Safety Evaluation (11th revision, 2021, SCCS/1628/21). Default values for fine fragrance / Eau de Parfum (leave-on) are applied to calculate the Systemic Exposure Dose (SED) for each ingredient.

Exposure ParameterValue

Product Categoryfine-fragrance
Product TypeLeave-on (non-pressurised spray)
Duration and FrequencyDaily; 1 application per day

Estimated Amount of Product Applied per Day240 mg/day (0.24 g/day — SCCS 2021 default for fine fragrance)
Target GroupAdults
Body Weight (BW)60 kg (conservative SCCS default)
Calculated Total Product SED(240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day
Calculation Formula & Focus Ingredients

SED (mg/kg bw/day) = (Amount applied [mg/day] × Ingredient concentration [fraction] × Retention Factor) / Body Weight [kg]

Total Product SED:
= (240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day

Example — Alcohol Denat. (75.00%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.7500 = 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day

Example — Vanillin (0.5053%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.005053 = 0.020212 mg/kg bw/day

Example — Coumarin (0.1503%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.001503 = 0.006012 mg/kg bw/day

Notes on Exposure Assessment

The total product SED of 4.0 mg/kg bw/day reflects dermal absorption of all non-volatile ingredients. Ethanol, as the primary solvent (75.00%), rapidly evaporates from the skin surface; however, a conservative dermal absorption factor of 100% is applied consistent with SCCS guidance for ethanol in cosmetics, yielding SED = 3.0 mg/kg bw/day.

The fragrance mixture (15.4586%) is assessed at the full formulated concentration per IFRA methodology. Purified water (4.97%) is not separately assessed — water presents no systemic toxicological concern. Trace colorants (CI 19140 and CI 17200) are present at <1% and are EU-approved per Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 Annex IV.

9. Toxicological Profile

ALCOHOL DENAT.

CAS Number: 64-17-5 | EC Number: 200-578-6 | Function: Solvent / Self-preserving | Concentration: 75.00% w/w

NOAEL: 2600 mg/kg bw/day | SED: 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day | MoS: 867

Ethanol (Alcohol Denat.) is a thoroughly characterised solvent with an extensive history of safe use in fine fragrances. 3. Reasoning

Basis for Safety Conclusion

The safety assessment of H&M Fearless Optimist Eau De Parfum 100 ml has been performed in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and the SCCS Notes of Guidance (11th revision, 2021, SCCS/1628/21). The reasoning supports the conclusion that this product is safe for human health when used as intended by adults.

Formulation: The product is an alcohol-based fine fragrance. All primary ingredients (Alcohol Denat. 75.00%, Parfum/Sol do Brasil mod 1 15.4586%, Aqua 4.97%, and trace colorants CI 19140 and CI 17200) are established cosmetic ingredients. The formulation does not contain any ingredients prohibited under Annex II of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. Coumarin, listed in Annex III, is present at 0.1503% and has been assessed with a MoS of 1,666 — confirming safety. All allergen concentrations are within safety limits.

Fragrance safety: The fragrance mixture Sol do Brasil mod 1 (P09K567) is IFRA 51st Amendment compliant at 15.4586% w/w, as confirmed by the IFRA 51st Amendment Certificate on file. No ingredients were identified with a Margin of Safety (MoS) below 100 using conservative safety defaults.

Allergen declaration: Five declared fragrance allergens exceed the 0.001% leave-on threshold per Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1545: Vanillin (0.5053%), Benzyl Salicylate (0.2975%), Hydroxycitronellal (0.1531%), Coumarin (0.1503%), and Linalool (0.0048%). Their identity and concentrations are confirmed via the INCI Composition Certificate (SIL/MFEDP/FO/002-00, dated 14.01.2026). All MoS values for these allergens significantly exceed the required threshold of 100 — confirming a high margin of safety for systemic exposure under intended conditions of use.

Stability and microbiological quality: The product demonstrates physicochemical stability over 9 months of testing at ambient (23–25°C) and 40°C. Four freeze-thaw cycles (−20°C / ambient) further confirm the robustness of the 100 ml format. The high ethanol content (75.00% w/w) renders the product inherently self-preserving; microbiological challenge testing per ISO 11930 is not required per SCCS guidelines for products containing ≥20% ethanol.

Flammability: The product is classified as Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) due to its flash point of ~13°C. This is a physical hazard addressed through appropriate handling instructions and label warnings, not a toxicological risk under intended use.
