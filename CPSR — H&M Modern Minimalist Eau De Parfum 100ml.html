The product is considered safe when used according to instructions; any misuse beyond these parameters is addressed by standard regulatory warnings for flammable fine fragrances.

7. Exposure to the Cosmetic Product

Exposure assessment follows the SCCS Notes of Guidance for the Testing of Cosmetic Ingredients and their Safety Evaluation (11th revision, 2021, SCCS/1628/21). Default values for fine fragrance / Eau de Parfum (leave-on) are applied to calculate the Systemic Exposure Dose (SED) for each ingredient.

Exposure ParameterValue
Areas of ApplicationSkin (body — neck, wrists, décolleté, pulse points)
Product Categoryfine-fragrance
Product TypeLeave-on (non-pressurised spray)
Duration and FrequencyDaily; 1 application per day
Retention Factor (RF)1.0 (leave-on product)
Estimated Amount of Product Applied per Day240 mg/day (0.24 g/day — SCCS 2021 default for fine fragrance)
Target GroupAdults
Body Weight (BW)60 kg (conservative SCCS default)
Calculated Total Product SED(240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day
Calculation Formula & Focus Ingredients

SED (mg/kg bw/day) = (Amount applied [mg/day] × Ingredient concentration [fraction] × Retention Factor) / Body Weight [kg]

Total Product SED:
= (240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day

Example — HEXAMETHYLINDANOPYRAN (2.67344%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.0267344 = 0.10694 mg/kg bw/day

Example — Alcohol Denat. (75.00%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.7500 = 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day

Notes on Exposure Assessment

The total product SED of 4.0 mg/kg bw/day reflects dermal absorption of all non-volatile ingredients. Ethanol, as the primary solvent (75.00%), rapidly evaporates from the skin surface; however, a conservative dermal absorption factor of 100% is applied consistent with SCCS guidance for ethanol in cosmetics, yielding SED = 3.0 mg/kg bw/day.

AHTN / OTNE Focus: As a significant constituent of the fragrance mixture (T Shirt Mod P01X566), Tetramethyl acetyloctahydronaphthalenes is assessed at its full concentration in the finished product (2.03076% w/w). Given its specific toxicological profile (NOAEL 12.5 mg/kg/d), the SED of 0.08123 mg/kg bw/day is a critical value for the Margin of Safety (MoS) calculation in Section 8.

The fragrance mixture (20.00%) is assessed at the full formulated concentration per IFRA methodology. Purified water (4.97%) is not separately assessed — water presents no systemic toxicological concern. Trace colorants (CI 42090 and CI 17200) are present at <1% and are EU-approved per Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 Annex IV.

9. Toxicological Profile

ALCOHOL DENAT.

CAS Number: 64-17-5 | EC Number: 200-578-6 | Function: Solvent / Self-preserving | Concentration: 75.00% w/w

NOAEL: 2600 mg/kg bw/day | SED: 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day | MoS: 867

Ethanol (Alcohol Denat.) is a thoroughly characterized solvent with an extensive history of safe use in fine fragrances. It is denatured with tert-butanol (TBA) and denatonium benzoate to render it unfit for consumption. The NOAEL for systemic toxicity is 2600 mg/kg bw/day based on SCCS (2012) evaluations. At 75.00% w/w in a fine fragrance with a daily exposure of 240 mg, the SED is 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day, yielding a MoS of 867—well above the 100 threshold. It is not classified as a skin sensitizer, though mild transient defatting of the skin may occur with repeated use. A flash point of ~13°C necessitates a Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) classification.

IFRA Certificate: IFRACERTIFICATEEU_GB_EN_P01X566_TSHIRTMOD_20260107_25688239 (51st Amendment)  |  MoS: N/A — IFRA Compliant

The fragrance mixture 'T Shirt Mod' is assessed at the formulated concentration of 20.00% w/w in Eau de Parfum. AQUA (Purified Water)

3. Reasoning

Basis for Safety Conclusion

The safety assessment of H&M Modern Minimalist Eau De Parfum 100 ml has been performed in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and the SCCS Notes of Guidance (11th revision, 2021, SCCS/1628/21). The reasoning supports the conclusion that this product is safe for human health when used as intended by adults.

Formulation: The product is an alcohol-based fine fragrance. All primary ingredients (Alcohol Denat. 75.00%, Parfum/T Shirt Mod 20.00%, Aqua 4.97%, and trace colorants CI 42090 and CI 17200) are established cosmetic ingredients. The formulation does not contain any ingredients prohibited under Annex II of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. The allergen concentrations are well within safety limits.

Fragrance safety: The fragrance mixture T Shirt Mod (P01X566) is IFRA 51st Amendment compliant at 20.00% w/w, confirmed by certificate IFRACERTIFICATEEU_GB_EN_P01X566_TSHIRTMOD_20260107_25688239. No ingredients were identified with a Margin of Safety (MoS) below 100 using conservative safety defaults.

Allergen declaration: The 22 declared fragrance allergens exceed the 0.001% leave-on threshold. Document "H&M EDP NEW RANGE 100 ml with alller.pdf" confirms their identity and concentrations. All identified allergens, including Hexamethylindanopyran (MoS = 374) and Tetramethyl Acetyloctahydronaphthalenes (MoS = 154), yield MoS values significantly higher than the required threshold of 100. This confirms a high margin of safety for systemic exposure under intended conditions of use.

Stability and microbiological quality: The product demonstrates physicochemical stability over 9 months of testing at 5°C, ambient (23–25°C), and 40°C. Four freeze-thaw cycles (−20°C / ambient) further confirm the robustness of the 100 ml format. The high ethanol content (75.00% w/w) renders the product inherently self-preserving; microbiological challenge testing per ISO 11930 is not required per SCCS guidelines for products containing ≥20% ethanol.

Flammability: The product is classified as Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) due to its flash point of ~13°C. This is a physical hazard addressed through appropriate handling instructions and label warnings, not a toxicological risk under intended use.
