7. Exposure to the Cosmetic Product

Exposure assessment follows the SCCS Notes of Guidance for the Testing of Cosmetic Ingredients and their Safety Evaluation (11th revision, 2021, SCCS/1628/21). Default values for fine fragrance / Eau de Parfum (leave-on) are applied to calculate the Systemic Exposure Dose (SED) for each ingredient.

Exposure ParameterValue

Product CategoryFine Fragrance / Eau de Parfum

Target GroupAdults
Body Weight (BW)60 kg (conservative SCCS default)
Calculated Total Product SED(240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day
Calculation Formula

SED (mg/kg bw/day) = (Amount applied [mg/day] × Ingredient concentration [fraction] × Retention Factor) / Body Weight [kg]

Total Product SED:
= (240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day

Per-Ingredient SED:
SEDingredient = 4.0 × (concentration fraction) = 4.0 × (% / 100)

Example — Alcohol Denat. (75.00%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.7500 = 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day

Example — AHTN (4.85982%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.0485982 = 0.194393 mg/kg bw/day

Notes on Exposure Assessment

The total product SED of 4.0 mg/kg bw/day reflects dermal absorption of all non-volatile ingredients. Ethanol, as the primary solvent (75%), rapidly evaporates from the skin surface; however, a conservative dermal absorption factor of 100% is applied consistent with SCCS guidance for ethanol in cosmetics, yielding SED = 3.0 mg/kg bw/day. The fragrance mixture (20.00%) is assessed at the full formulated concentration per IFRA methodology. Aqua (5.00%) is not separately assessed — water presents no systemic toxicological concern.

ALCOHOL DENAT.

CAS Number: 64-17-5  |  EC Number: 200-578-6  |  Function: Solvent / Self-preserving  |  Concentration: 75.00% w/w

NOAEL: 2600 mg/kg bw/day  |  SED: 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day  |  MoS: 867

Ethanol (Alcohol Denat., CAS 64-17-5) is one of the most thoroughly characterised solvents with an extensive history of safe use in cosmetic products, including fine fragrances at concentrations above 70% w/w. The substance is denatured with tert-butanol (TBA) and denatonium benzoate to render it unfit for consumption, as required by applicable regulations. The NOAEL for systemic toxicity is 2600 mg/kg bw/day based on SCCS (2012) and EFSA Panel on Food Contact Materials evaluations. At 75.00% w/w in a fine fragrance with daily exposure of 240 mg, the SED is 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day, yielding a MoS of 867 — far above the 100 threshold. Ethanol has no genotoxic, carcinogenic or reproductive toxicity concerns associated with topical cosmetic exposure at this level. It is not classified as a skin sensitiser. Mild transient defatting of the skin may occur with repeated use; this is acceptable at the usage frequency and area of application for fine fragrance. The ethanol is confirmed by CoA at 75.22% v/v. Flash point ~13°C necessitates Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) classification; this is a physical hazard, not a toxicological concern under normal use conditions.

3. Reasoning

Basis for Safety Conclusion

The safety assessment of H&M Pink Dawn Eau De Parfum 50 ml has been performed in accordance with the requirements of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, Annex I, and the SCCS Notes of Guidance for the Testing of Cosmetic Ingredients and their Safety Evaluation (11th revision, 2021, SCCS/1628/21). The following reasoning supports the conclusion that this product is safe for human health when used as intended by adults, subject to the stated conditions of safe use.

Formulation: The product is a 3-ingredient alcohol-based fine fragrance (Eau de Parfum) formulation. All primary ingredients (Alcohol Denat. 75.00%, Parfum/Blow Kiss RW 20.00%, Aqua 5.00%) are established cosmetic actives with well-documented safety profiles and long histories of safe use. The formulation does not contain any ingredients prohibited under Annex II of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. Coumarin (present as a declared allergen at 0.243% w/w) is a restricted substance under Annex III; its presence is within IFRA 51st Amendment limits and represents an acceptable concentration in an Eau de Parfum formulation.

Fragrance safety and AHTN: The fragrance mixture Blow Kiss RW (P02W690) is IFRA 51st Amendment compliant at 20.00% w/w, confirmed by certificate IFRACERTIFICATEEU_IN_EN_P02W690_BLOWKISSRW2_20251003_25288955. The principal point of safety note is the AHTN concentration of 4.85982% w/w in the finished product. Using the conservative SCCS/1543/14 NOAEL of 12.5 mg/kg bw/day and SCCS default exposure values, the MoS calculates to 64 — below the conventional 100 threshold. However, SCCS/1543/14 acknowledges significant residual uncertainty in this NOAEL, and IFRA limits for AHTN in Eau de Parfum are set to account for aggregate multi-product exposure scenarios. The product's IFRA compliance is confirmed. This CPSR notes this as a residual risk item requiring post-market vigilance and confirmation of AHTN category-specific compliance by the manufacturer.

Allergen declaration: All 22 declared fragrance allergens exceed the 0.001% leave-on threshold and are required to be declared on the product label per Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1545. The EU 80-allergen declaration document FRAG_ALLERG_80_GB_EN_P02W690_BLOWKISSRW_20260107_25688233.pdf confirms allergen identity and concentration. All 22 allergens have been individually assessed in Section 9; all except AHTN yield MoS values well in excess of 100.

Stability and microbiological quality: The product demonstrates excellent physicochemical stability over 9 months of testing at 5°C, ambient (23–25°C) and 40°C, with all parameters (packaging, appearance, fragrance, colour, alcohol content, vacuum leak, specific gravity) remaining within specification at all time points. Four freeze-thaw cycles at −20°C confirm robustness under extreme thermal stress. The high ethanol content (75.00% w/w) renders the product inherently self-preserving; microbiological challenge testing per ISO 11930 is not required.

Flammability: The product is correctly classified as Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) per Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 (CLP) due to its flash point of approximately 13°C. Comprehensive GHS/CLP hazard labelling is applied. This classification reflects a physical hazard requiring safe handling; it does not affect toxicological safety under normal cosmetic use conditions when the product is kept away from ignition sources as directed.
