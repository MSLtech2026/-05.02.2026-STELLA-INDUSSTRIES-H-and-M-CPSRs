7. Exposure to the Cosmetic Product

Exposure assessment follows the SCCS Notes of Guidance for the Testing of Cosmetic Ingredients and their Safety Evaluation (11th revision, 2021, SCCS/1628/21). Default values for fine fragrance / Eau de Parfum (leave-on) are applied to calculate the Systemic Exposure Dose (SED) for each ingredient.

Exposure ParameterValue
Areas of ApplicationPulse points — neck, wrists, décolleté
Product CategoryFine Fragrance / Eau de Parfum
Product TypeLeave-on (pump spray)
Duration and FrequencyDaily; 1 application per day
Retention Factor (RF)1.0 (leave-on product)
Estimated Amount of Product Applied per Day240 mg/day (0.24 g/day — SCCS 2021 default for fine fragrance)
Target GroupAdults
Body Weight (BW)60 kg (conservative SCCS default)
Calculated Total Product SED(240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day
Calculation Formula

SED (mg/kg bw/day) = (Amount applied [mg/day] × Ingredient concentration [fraction] × Retention Factor) / Body Weight [kg]

Total Product SED:
= (240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day

Per-Ingredient SED:
SEDingredient = 4.0 × (concentration fraction) = 4.0 × (% / 100)

Example — Alcohol Denat. (75.00%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.7500 = 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day

Example — Vanillin (0.0505%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.000505 = 0.00202 mg/kg bw/day

Notes on Exposure Assessment

The total product SED of 4.0 mg/kg bw/day reflects dermal absorption of all non-volatile ingredients. Ethanol, as the primary solvent (75%), rapidly evaporates from the skin surface; however, a conservative dermal absorption factor of 100% is applied consistent with SCCS guidance for ethanol in cosmetics, yielding SED = 3.0 mg/kg bw/day. The fragrance mixture (20.00%) is assessed at the full formulated concentration per IFRA methodology. Aqua (5.00%) is not separately assessed — water presents no systemic toxicological concern.

9. Toxicological Profile

ALCOHOL DENAT.

CAS Number: 64-17-5 | EC Number: 200-578-6 | Function: Solvent / Self-preserving | Concentration: 75.00% w/w

NOAEL: 2600 mg/kg bw/day | SED: 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day | MoS: 867

Ethanol (Alcohol Denat.) is a thoroughly characterized solvent with an extensive history of safe use in fine fragrances. It is denatured with tert-butanol (TBA) and denatonium benzoate to render it unfit for consumption. The NOAEL for systemic toxicity is 2600 mg/kg bw/day based on SCCS (2012) evaluations. At 75.00% w/w in a fine fragrance with a daily exposure of 240 mg, the SED is 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day, yielding a MoS of 867—well above the 100 threshold. It is not classified as a skin sensitizer, though mild transient defatting of the skin may occur with repeated use. A flash point of ~13°C necessitates a Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) classification.

PARFUM (Nuage Blanc RW — P02R790)

Trade Name: Nuage Blanc RW  |  Supplier Code: P02R790  |  Function: Masking / Fragrance  |  Concentration: 20.00% w/w

IFRA Certificate: IFRACERTIFICATE_P02R790_NUAGE_BLANC_RW_20260107 (51st Amendment)  |  MoS: N/A — IFRA Compliant

The fragrance mixture Nuage Blanc RW (P02R790) is assessed at the formulated concentration of 20.00% w/w in Eau de Parfum. The IFRA 51st Amendment Certificate confirms compliance with all IFRA category limits at the stated usage level[cite: 25, 41]. The allergen declaration identifies the presence and concentration of fragrance allergens (Vanillin, Citral, Geraniol, and Citronellol) above the 0.001% leave-on threshold. All declared allergens are separately profiled. The fragrance mixture is assessed as safe at the formulated level, subject to IFRA compliance and allergen labelling requirements[cite: 25, 33]. No prohibited or CMR fragrance ingredients have been identified in the allergen declaration or IFRA certificate at concerning concentrations[cite: 25, 26].

AQUA (Purified Water)

CAS Number: 7732-18-5  |  EC Number: 231-791-2  |  Function: Carrier / Solvent  |  Concentration: 5.00% w/w

Assessment: Not assessed — intrinsically safe

It functions as a carrier in this formulation at 5.00% w/w. No NOAEL assessment or MoS calculation is required[cite: 25, 33]. Water presents no dermal, systemic or mutagenic risk and requires no further toxicological evaluation[cite: 25, 33].

VANILLIN

Vanillin is a widely used fragrance and flavor component with a well-established safety profile. Based on its concentration of 0.0505% in this formulation, the systemic exposure is negligible. 3. Reasoning

Basis for Safety Conclusion

The safety assessment of H&M Silk Stone Eau De Parfum 50 ml has been performed in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and the SCCS Notes of Guidance (12th revision, 2023, SCCS/1647/22). The reasoning supports the conclusion that this product is safe for human health when used as intended by adults.

Formulation: The product is an alcohol-based fine fragrance. All primary ingredients (Alcohol Denat. 75.00%, Parfum/Nuage Blanc RW 20.00%, Aqua 5.00%) are established cosmetic ingredients. The formulation does not contain any ingredients prohibited under Annex II of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. The allergen concentrations are well within safety limits.

Fragrance safety: The fragrance mixture Nuage Blanc RW (P02R790) is IFRA 51st Amendment compliant at 20.00% w/w, confirmed by certificate IFRACERTIFICATE_P02R790_NUAGE_BLANC_RW_20260107. Unlike other variants in the range, no ingredients were identified with an MoS below 100 using conservative safety defaults.

Allergen declaration: The 4 declared fragrance allergens (Vanillin, Citral, Geraniol, Citronellol) exceed the 0.001% leave-on threshold. Document "H&M EDP NEW RANGE 50 ml with alller.pdf" confirms their concentrations. All 4 allergens yield MoS values significantly higher than 100, indicating a high margin of safety for systemic exposure.

Stability and microbiological quality: The product demonstrates physicochemical stability over 6 months of accelerated testing at 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5%. The high ethanol content (75.00% w/w) renders the product inherently self-preserving; microbiological challenge testing per ISO 11930 is not required per SCCS guidelines for low-risk products.

Flammability: The product is classified as Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) due to its flash point of ~13°C. This is a physical hazard addressed through appropriate handling instructions and label warnings, not a toxicological risk under intended use.
