Reasonably Foreseeable Misuse

Reasonably foreseeable misuse scenarios include: application to the face or broken skin; use by children; inhalation of spray mist at close range; ingestion; use near open flames. The risk from each scenario is addressed through product labelling, childproof packaging (crimped pump) and the conditions of safe use detailed in Part B. No such misuse would render the product unsafe when the conditions of safe use are disregarded, beyond the known risks common to high-alcohol fine fragrances.

7. Exposure to the Cosmetic Product

Exposure assessment follows the SCCS Notes of Guidance for the Testing of Cosmetic Ingredients and their Safety Evaluation (11th revision, 2021, SCCS/1628/21). Default values for fine fragrance / Eau de Parfum (leave-on) are applied to calculate the Systemic Exposure Dose (SED) for each ingredient.

Exposure ParameterValue
Areas of ApplicationPulse points — neck, wrists, décolleté
Product CategoryFine Fragrance / Eau de Parfum
Product TypeLeave-on (pump spray)
Duration and FrequencyDaily; 1 application per day
Retention Factor (RF)1.0 (leave-on product)
Estimated Amount of Product Applied per Day240 mg/day (0.24 g/day — SCCS 2021 default for fine fragrance)
Target GroupAdults
Body Weight (BW)60 kg (conservative SCCS default)
Calculated Total Product SED(240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day
Calculation Formula & Focus Ingredients

SED (mg/kg bw/day) = (Amount applied [mg/day] × Ingredient concentration [fraction] × Retention Factor) / Body Weight [kg]

Total Product SED:
= (240 mg × 1.0) / 60 kg = 4.0 mg product / kg bw / day

Example — OTNE / ATHN (3.47826%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.0347826 = 0.13913 mg/kg bw/day

Example — Alcohol Denat. (75.00%):
SED = 4.0 × 0.7500 = 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day

Notes on Exposure Assessment

The total product SED of 4.0 mg/kg bw/day reflects dermal absorption of all non-volatile ingredients. Ethanol, as the primary solvent (75%), rapidly evaporates from the skin surface; however, a conservative dermal absorption factor of 100% is applied consistent with SCCS guidance for ethanol in cosmetics, yielding SED = 3.0 mg/kg bw/day.

OTNE / ATHN Focus: As a significant constituent of the fragrance mixture (Summer Stone Flower P02W715), Tetramethyl acetyloctahydronaphthalenes is assessed at its full concentration in the finished product (3.47826% w/w). Given its specific toxicological profile (NOAEL 12.5 mg/kg/d), the SED of 0.13913 mg/kg bw/day is a critical value for the Margin of Safety (MoS) calculation in Section 8.

The fragrance mixture (20.00%) is assessed at the full formulated concentration per IFRA methodology. Aqua (5.00%) is not separately assessed — water presents no systemic toxicological concern.

9. Toxicological Profile

ALCOHOL DENAT.

CAS Number: 64-17-5 | EC Number: 200-578-6 | Function: Solvent / Self-preserving | Concentration: 75.00% w/w

NOAEL: 2600 mg/kg bw/day | SED: 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day | MoS: 867

Ethanol (Alcohol Denat.) is a thoroughly characterized solvent with an extensive history of safe use in fine fragrances. It is denatured with tert-butanol (TBA) and denatonium benzoate to render it unfit for consumption. The NOAEL for systemic toxicity is 2600 mg/kg bw/day based on SCCS (2012) evaluations. At 75.00% w/w in a fine fragrance with a daily exposure of 240 mg, the SED is 3.0000 mg/kg bw/day, yielding a MoS of 867—well above the 100 threshold. It is not classified as a skin sensitizer, though mild transient defatting of the skin may occur with repeated use. A flash point of ~13°C necessitates a Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) classification.

3. Reasoning

Basis for Safety Conclusion

The safety assessment of H&M Solar Blur Eau De Parfum 50 ml has been performed in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and the SCCS Notes of Guidance (12th revision, 2023, SCCS/1647/22). The reasoning supports the conclusion that this product is safe for human health when used as intended by adults.

Formulation: The product is an alcohol-based fine fragrance. All primary ingredients (Alcohol Denat. 75.00%, Parfum/SUMMER STONE FLOWER P02W71520.00%, Aqua 5.00%) are established cosmetic ingredients. The formulation does not contain any ingredients prohibited under Annex II of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. The allergen concentrations are well within safety limits.

Fragrance safety: The fragrance mixture SUMMER STONE FLOWER P02W715 is IFRA 51st Amendment compliant at 20.00% w/w, confirmed by certificate IFRACERTIFICATEEU_IN_EN_P02W715_SUMMERSTONEFLOWER_20251003_25288949. Except OTNE, no ingredients were identified with an MoS below 100 using conservative safety defaults.

Allergen declaration: The 20 declared fragrance allergens exceed the 0.001% leave-on threshold. Document "H&M EDP NEW RANGE 50 ml with alller.pdf" confirms their concentrations. With the exception of OTNE, which yields a Margin of Safety (MoS) of 90 based on conservative SCCS defaults, all identified allergens yield MoS values significantly higher than 100. The safety of OTNE at this concentration (3.47826%) is supported by its IFRA 51st Amendment compliance for Category 4 products, confirming a high margin of safety for systemic exposure under intended conditions of use.

Stability and microbiological quality: The product demonstrates physicochemical stability over 9 months of testing at 5°C, ambient and 40°C. Four freeze-thaw cycles confirm robustness. The high ethanol content (75.00% w/w) renders the product inherently self-preserving; microbiological challenge testing per ISO 11930 is not required per SCCS guidelines for low-risk products.

Flammability: The product is classified as Flammable Liquid Category 2 (H225) due to its flash point of ~13°C. This is a physical hazard addressed through appropriate handling instructions and label warnings, not a toxicological risk under intended use.
